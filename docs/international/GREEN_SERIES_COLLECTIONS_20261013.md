# Collection architecture — internal release record

The seven existing series, their English labels, colours, images and typography are unchanged. Collection definitions and verified book-ID membership live in `catalog/collections.cjs`. Every English catalogue book now has one `primaryEditionLine`, one `primaryCollection`, a `topics` array, an optional `workGroupId`, and a `publicationStatus` derived from existing availability. No cover, ISBN, release date or price is generated by this extension.

| Green Series collection | Route | State and verified contents |

| British Empire Collection | `/collections/green-series/british-empire/` | Public; six existing forthcoming English editions and three published German editions |
| American Military History | `/collections/green-series/american-military-history/` | Public; *The Rough Riders* forthcoming |

| Exploration & Frontier Worlds | `/collections/green-series/exploration-frontier-worlds/` | Public additional subseries requested by the publisher; existing Everest and Grey Owl pages, both forthcoming |

The common collection registry covers all seven series. Books in the other six series are temporarily placed in each line's private `editorial-review` collection until their thematic collections are checked against the real stock. Those review buckets have no public route or link. A series page shows “Explore the Collections” only when at least two populated public collections exist. The Green Series meets that condition; the other six currently do not.

The inherited English series data already identifies GREEN with “Military, Expeditions, and Frontier Regions” and ANTHRACITE with “World Literature and Elemental Conflict”; the old green-as-natural-history / anthracite-as-military arrangement is not present in this source. The Green Series still contains Everest and Grey Owl, now accurately distinguished from the military collections in the publisher-requested additional subseries. No main-series reassignment was made.

Production source is the international Eleventy tree. At the time of this work, remote `origin/main` lacked `international/`; the clean worktree was advanced to the checked English website branch `origin/codex/haggard-en-website-sync-20261013` before edits. Deployment remains a separate, hash-bound publisher decision.
