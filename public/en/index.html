Standards

✦ SERIES

Six series, one measure.

AETERNUS VERLAG does not order its publications according to passing market moods, but according to enduring fields of substance. Each series follows a clear intellectual focus and a distinct visual colour.

I ANTHRACITE

World Literature and Elemental Conflict

Weighty world literature of the frontier and the primal conflict — from Melville to Simms — alongside historical accounts of conquest and campaign.

II GREEN

Military, Expeditions, and Frontier Regions

Campaigns, special operations, military memoirs, frontier wars, and the long shadow of armed order.

III RED

Empires, Rulers, and Statecraft

Empires, republics, administration, power, law, diplomatic order, and historical sources.

IV BLUE

Economy, Industry, and Titans

The rise and work of the great industrialists and entrepreneurs — capital formation, competition, and the shaping of the modern economy.

V AMBER

Youth, Adventure, and Character Formation

Classic adventure literature, education through nature, courage, responsibility, and the early encounter with the seriousness of life.

VI WHITE

The Northern World

Sagas, seafaring, discovery, and everyday life in the North — from polar exploration to the traditions of Scandinavia and Iceland.

✦ TRANSLATION

Making older texts readable again.

At AETERNUS VERLAG, translation is not a secondary production step. It is the core of the edition. Anyone who brings historical literature, travel accounts, military memoirs, nature writing, or early economic and political texts back into print faces a double task: the text must become intelligible to modern readers without losing its historical soul.

— AETERNUS does not make old texts younger. AETERNUS makes them accessible again.
